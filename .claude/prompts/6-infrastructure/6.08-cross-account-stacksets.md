# Prompt: Cross-account Spoke Roles (StackSets)

## Goal
Deploy spoke execution roles to DEV/STG/PROD target accounts from the management account.

## Constraints
- `infrastructure/management/spoke-roles-stackset.yaml` deploys `spoke-{env}-execution-role` per
  target account.
- Trust: only platform-worker-task-role, `sts:ExternalId` per env.
- Permissions: superset of per-template needs; per-template roles nest within the spoke.
- StackSet auto-deploy on new accounts added to the target OU. Deployed once from the
  management-account boot pipeline.

## Acceptance
Adding a new account to the OU auto-provisions the spoke role within 15 minutes.

## Enhancements (v2.1)
- Use **service-managed permissions with delegated administrator** (not the management account's
  own credentials day-to-day) — CI should never hold management-account deploy rights.
- Spoke role sessions constrained: `sts:SourceIdentity` required and propagated
  (`platform-request-{id}`) so CloudTrail in target accounts attributes every action to a request.
- Spoke role also carries the permissions boundary; boundary itself deployed by the same StackSet.
