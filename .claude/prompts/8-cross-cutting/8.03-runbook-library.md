# Prompt: Runbook Library

## Goal
Produce `docs/runbooks/*.md` — at least 10 runbooks covering the paging alerts.

## Constraints
- Runbooks: dlq-depth, api-5xx, worker-not-processing, slo-burn, rds-connection-saturation,
  sync-stuck, stack-stuck-in-progress, event-lag-rising, sts-assume-role-failures, cost-anomaly.
- Template per the observability spec: what it means, severity, detection, triage, immediate
  actions, recovery, root cause, post-incident.
- Every command block copy-pasteable. Cross-linked to dashboards and neighboring runbooks.

## Acceptance
Each runbook reviewed by at least one engineer who did not write it.

## Enhancements (v2.1)
- Commands are least-privilege and read-only in the triage section; anything mutating is fenced
  in "Immediate actions" with an explicit "requires role X" note — on-call at 3am copies blindly.
- Each runbook's frontmatter lists `verified_against` (env + date of last live drill) — pairs with
  the 4.12 quarterly alert test.
