# Prompt: Recording Rules (SLIs)

## Goal
Produce `observability/rules/recording.yaml`.

## Constraints
- Rules per SLI: sli:api_availability:ratio_rate_5m, sli:api_availability:ratio_rate_30m,
  sli:request_submit_latency_p99:5m, sli:time_to_create_complete_p95_5m{template},
  sli:event_lag_p99_5m, sli:approval_duration_p95_1d.
- Multi-window burn-rate rules per the Google SRE Workbook: 1h and 6h burn ratios.
- Evaluation interval 30 s. Deployed to AMP via `aws amp create-rule-groups-namespace`.

## Acceptance
Alert rules read only recording rules, never raw metrics.

## Enhancements (v2.1)
- SLO targets declared in one `slo.yaml` (target %, window) and templated into rules — targets
  aren't scattered as magic numbers.
- `promtool check rules` (or amp-compatible equivalent) runs in CI on every rules change.
