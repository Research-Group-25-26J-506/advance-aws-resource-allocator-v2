# Prompt: Platform Metrics Catalogue

## Goal
Register all custom platform metrics in `backend/observability`.

## Constraints
- **Counters:** platform_requests_submitted_total{template,env,region,source};
  platform_requests_completed_total{template,env,status}; platform_cfn_calls_total{operation,result};
  platform_sts_assume_role_total{target_account,result}; platform_template_publishes_total{template,result};
  platform_template_sync_total{result,actor_role}; platform_templates_synced_total{action};
  platform_template_validation_failures_total{stage,template}; platform_drift_detected_total{template};
  platform_idempotency_hits_total.
- **Gauges:** platform_approvals_pending{env}; platform_worker_inflight;
  platform_template_sync_inflight; platform_dlq_depth{queue}.
- **Histograms:** platform_request_duration_seconds{template,env,terminal_status};
  platform_approval_duration_seconds{env}; platform_worker_poll_duration_seconds;
  platform_cfn_call_duration_seconds{operation}; platform_event_lag_seconds{event_type};
  platform_build_duration_seconds{repo,result}; platform_template_sync_duration_seconds{result}.
- Cardinality bounds: template ≤50; env ∈ {dev,stg,prod}; region ≤5; result/status enums ≤10.
  **No request_id, actor_id, or stack_id in labels.**
- Bucket definitions in a shared HistogramConfig bean; SLO-aligned.
- Every metric documented in `docs/metrics.md`.

## Acceptance
`count by (__name__)({__name__=~"platform_.*"})` shows exactly the catalogued metrics.
Cardinality per metric under 10k series.

## Enhancements (v2.1)
- A unit test iterates registered meters and asserts label sets against the catalogue — drift
  between code and docs/metrics.md fails the build.
- `target_account` label is the account **alias** (bounded set), never raw account IDs typed by users.
- dlq_depth gauge sourced from SQS `ApproximateNumberOfMessagesVisible` poll every 30s in the
  worker (single writer, avoids duplicate series from api).
