# Prompt: Cardinality Watchdog

## Goal
Add a scheduled Lambda that queries AMP for high-cardinality metrics and alerts on threshold breach.

## Constraints
- Runs weekly. Query: `count by (__name__) ({__name__=~"platform_.*"})`.
- Alert if any metric exceeds 10,000 series or grew > 50% week-over-week.
- Report to Slack #platform-info with top 10 metrics by cardinality; publish INFO alert to
  Alertmanager.

## Acceptance
Adding a request_id label to a metric surfaces within a week and triggers the alert.

## Enhancements (v2.1)
- Store weekly snapshots in DynamoDB (metric → series count) so week-over-week growth doesn't
  depend on AMP retention math and history is graphable.
- Also watch **label-value counts** per metric (`count(count by (label)(metric))`) for the
  catalogued high-risk labels (template, target_account) — total series can hide one exploding label.
